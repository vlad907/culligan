FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things North State homeowners ask us most. Don't see yours? Call or book a free water test.

How do I know if I have hard water?
Spots on dishes and glassware, soap that won't lather, dry skin and hair, and scale on faucets are the classic signs. A free in-home water test tells you exactly what's in your water.
What's the difference between a softener and a conditioner?
A softener removes hardness minerals using salt; a salt-free conditioner reduces scale without adding sodium. We'll recommend the right fit for your home and water.
Is the water test really free?
Yes — a Culligan expert tests your water on-site and explains the results in plain English, with no cost and no pressure.
Do you service commercial and industrial accounts?
We do — including deionization, water testing, and treatment for restaurants, ag, and manufacturing across Butte, Glenn, and Tehama counties.
Can I pay my bill or manage my account online?
Yes. Use the Pay My Bill portal to view and pay invoices and manage your account anytime.
Do you deliver salt?
We do — scheduled salt delivery plus service and maintenance, so your system keeps running perfectly between visits.
